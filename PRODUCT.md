# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Property seekers, landlords, agents, estate managers, and administrators using a verified Nigerian property marketplace. Visitors need to understand a listing quickly and move into the correct next step: apply, book, make an enquiry, schedule an inspection, or message the responsible person.

## Product Purpose

PropatiBox helps people discover, verify, transact around, and manage property in Nigeria. Success means that each listing leads users into a clear, trustworthy workflow whose data, permissions, and status remain consistent across public pages and role dashboards.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, clear, locally grounded. The product should feel confident and practical, reducing uncertainty around property discovery and follow-up without creating pressure or ambiguity.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic marketplace CTAs that hide the real workflow, opaque status changes, duplicated messaging surfaces, and visual patterns that feel like an unbranded real-estate template. Avoid decorative motion or styling that competes with the property, price, and next action.

## Design Principles

- Make the next property-specific action obvious.
- Preserve context and progress across authentication and profile completion.
- Reuse one source of truth for conversations, applications, bookings, inspections, and statuses.
- Make trust visible through clear ownership, validation, and confirmation states.
- Keep public discovery lightweight while making transactional steps explicit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Maintain visible focus states, keyboard-operable controls, readable contrast, descriptive labels, accessible dialogs and form errors, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support. Avoid relying on color alone for status or validation.
