# PROPATI — Public Page Design Overrides
Overrides `design-system/MASTER.md` for public-facing pages only.

## Allowed deviations
- Hero sections may use darkened photographic backgrounds with gradient overlay.
- Marketing copy may use warmer, shorter sentences than dashboard UI.
- Testimonials may include portrait images with soft shadow-2 treatment.
- CTA bands may use `bg-primary` with `text-on-primary` inversion.

## Required elements (per page)
- At least one trust signal (NBA, escrow, FIRS, verified badge).
- At least one local marker (city name, Naira pricing, Nigerian context).
- Mobile menu must be functional and not collapse into hamburger-only UX.
