# PropTech Operating System (Nigeria) — Unified Real Estate Infrastructure Platform

## Vision

A unified PropTech platform that functions as the **operating system for real estate in Nigeria**, connecting and coordinating all actors in the property ecosystem:

- Landlords / Property Owners  
- Tenants (Residential + Commercial)  
- Agents (Individuals)  
- Realtors  
- Real Estate Companies / Management Firms  
- Law Firms (Legal Validation Network)  
- Property Types: Residential, Short-let, Commercial Retail, Office Space, Warehouses / Industrial Assets  

The platform digitizes and standardizes:
- Property listings and management  
- Rent collection and booking systems  
- Legal agreements and compliance  
- Identity verification  
- Payments and receipts  
- Property operations and enforcement  

---

# 1. Core System Design Principle

Every property and transaction in the system is defined by:

## 1. Property Class
- Residential (long-term rental)
- Short-let (daily / weekly / monthly stays)
- Commercial Retail (shops, malls, kiosks)
- Office Space (corporate leases, coworking, private offices)
- Industrial / Warehousing (storage, logistics, manufacturing)

## 2. Management Type
- Self-managed (Landlord controlled)
- Agent-managed (Individual agent)
- Company-managed (Property management firms / estate companies)

## 3. Legal Mode
- Standard template (pre-approved contracts)
- Law-firm certified contracts (verified legal network)

---

# 2. Stakeholder System

## 2.1 Landlords / Property Owners
- List properties across all classes
- Set rent, pricing, availability, and terms
- Receive payments and reports
- Approve tenants or bookings
- Access legal documents and compliance records

---

## 2.2 Tenants (All Property Types)

Tenants include:
- Residential tenants
- Business tenants (shops, offices, warehouses)
- Short-let guests

They can:
- Search and filter properties by type
- Complete digital identity verification
- Sign contracts or booking agreements
- Make payments (rent or booking fees)
- Access receipts and documents
- Build verified rental history profile

---

## 2.3 Agents (Individuals)
- List and source properties
- Match tenants/clients with properties
- Manage viewings and bookings
- Earn commissions per transaction
- Manage client relationships and deals

---

## 2.4 Realtors
- Focus on property sales (buy/sell)
- Manage buyers and sellers
- Handle property acquisition pipelines
- Generate sale agreements
- Track commissions and deal flow

---

## 2.5 Real Estate Companies / Property Managers
- Multi-user organizational accounts
- Manage multiple properties across portfolios
- Assign agents/realtors under sub-accounts
- Control pricing, operations, and leasing
- Oversee maintenance and tenant management
- Manage financial reporting and commissions

Example structure:
- Company Account
  - Admin
  - Agents (sub-accounts)
  - Realtors (sub-accounts)
  - Operations staff

---

## 2.6 Law Firms (Legal Network Layer)
Law firms operate as an embedded marketplace providing:
- Contract review and approval
- Legal certification of tenancy and lease agreements
- Dispute resolution and arbitration
- Compliance validation (jurisdiction-based)

They are not embedded as employees, but as **independent network partners**.

# 4. Core Platform Infrastructure

## 4.1 Digital Identity System
Every participant is verified:
- Landlords
- Tenants
- Agents
- Companies
- Law firms

Verification includes:
- Government ID (NIN/BVN where applicable)
- Phone/email verification
- Business registration (CAC for companies)

---

## 4.2 Rent & Payment System
- Automated rent collection
- Booking payments (short-let)
- Service charge handling
- Payment splits (owners, agents, platform)
- Automated receipts
- Transaction history ledger

---

## 4.3 Digital Documentation Engine
All property interactions generate structured documents:
- Tenancy agreements
- Lease agreements
- Short-let booking contracts
- Agency agreements
- Inspection reports

All documents are:
- Digitally stored
- Version-controlled
- Searchable
- Access-controlled

---

## 4.4 Agreement & Legal Workflow System

### Step 1: Document Generation
Platform generates draft based on structured data

### Step 2: Law Firm Validation
- Routed to law firms (multi-firm ecosystem)
- Review, edit, approve or reject
- Jurisdiction compliance check

### Step 3: Digital Signing
- All parties sign digitally
- Timestamp + identity verification

### Step 4: Stamp Duty Integration
- Automated calculation and payment
- Digital stamp attached to contract

### Step 5: Activation
- Contract becomes legally active
- Rent or booking system is activated

---

## 4.5 Enforcement & Evidence Layer

The system maintains a full legal evidence trail:
- Signed contracts
- Payment records
- Booking history
- Tenant/landlord actions
- Audit logs

### Dispute Handling:
1. Internal resolution view (structured evidence)
2. Law firm arbitration
3. Exportable court-ready evidence pack
