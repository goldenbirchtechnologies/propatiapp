How verification works
01

Document Upload

Landlord uploads title deed, C of O, survey plan, or deed of assignment. Stored securely and flagged for review.

02

Identity Match

Name on property documents must match a government-issued ID — NIN, passport, or driver's licence.

03

Live Photo / Video Proof

Landlord records a short video inside the property holding a unique PROPATI QR code — impossible to fake with old photos.

04

Agent Physical Inspection

A registered PROPATI agent visits and counter-signs the verification. Unlocks the "Inspected" badge.

05

Community Flagging

Any user can flag a suspicious listing. Repeated flags trigger immediate manual review and temporary suspension.
