Platform features

Everything under one roof

💳

Online Rent Collection

Accept monthly rent payments online. Automated reminders keep tenants on track, and landlords get instant notifications when funds are received.

📄

Digitized Agreements

Create, send, and sign rental agreements electronically. Legally binding e-signatures mean no printing, scanning, or chasing signatures.

📹

Video & Voice Screening

Landlords can interview prospective tenants via built-in video or voice calls before making any decisions — all from within the platform.

🏘️

Multi-Property Management

Landlords can manage an unlimited number of properties from a single account — each with its own rent schedule, tenants, and documents.

🏷️

Short-let & Long-term Rental Modes

Automatically adapts rent schedules, agreements, and screening flows based on the rental type. Switch between modes with a single toggle.

How it works

From listing to lease
in four steps

01

List Your Property

Landlord or agent adds a property, sets the rental type (short-let or long-term), and uploads details.

02

Screen Tenants

Invite prospective tenants to a video or voice screening call directly through the platform.

03

Sign Agreement

Both parties sign the digitized rental agreement online — no paperwork, no delays.

04

Collect Rent

Monthly rent is collected automatically, with reminders for tenants and instant notifications for landlords.
