Reference: PROP-2024-88492-L

THIS LEASE AGREEMENT (the "Lease") is made this 24th day of May, 2024.

1. PARTIES

BETWEEN: Lumina Tech Holdings Ltd (the "Landlord") AND Vertex Global Logistics (the "Tenant").

verified Verified Clause

2. THE PREMISES

The Landlord hereby leases to the Tenant the commercial space located at Floor 12, Wing B, Eko Tower, Victoria Island, Lagos. The Premises comprise approximately 4,500 square feet of Grade-A office space, including five (5) designated parking stalls.

3. TERM

The term of this Lease shall be for a period of thirty-six (36) months, commencing on the 1st day of June, 2024 (the "Commencement Date") and terminating on the 31st day of May, 2027.

verified Verified Clause

4. RENT AND DEPOSIT

The annual base rent shall be ₦45,000,000.00 (Forty-Five Million Naira) payable annually in advance. A security deposit of ₦7,500,000.00 shall be held by the Landlord for the duration of the term.

5. PERMITTED USE

The Premises shall be used solely for general corporate office purposes and for no other purpose without the prior written consent of the Landlord.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

The Landlord shall be responsible for structural repairs including the roof, exterior walls, and common areas. The Tenant shall be responsible for all internal non-structural repairs and day-to-day maintenance of the interior Premises.
